More gaming feeling

1. Floating "+XP" number that flies from the quest row into the HUD XP bar on completion.
2. Perfect-day bonus: completing all dailies gives extra XP + special confetti ("DAILY
   CLEAR!").
3. Titles per level ("Novice" → "Adept" → "Master…") shown next to the role.
4. Achievement rarity tiers (common/rare/epic/legendary) with colored glows.
5. XP bar near-level state: when ≥90%, medallion pulses / bar glows harder.
6. Streak flame scales with streak length (7+ bigger, 30+ blue flame); streak-freeze token as
   a rare reward.
7. Optional sound effects (quest tick, level-up fanfare) with a mute toggle.
8. Character avatar/portrait in HudBanner that evolves with level.
9. Daily login reward calendar (7-day cycle, day 7 = chest).
10. Random loot drop on milestone completion (cosmetic: banner themes, avatar frames).
11. Weekly "boss quest" — one big challenge with a big XP payout and its own card.
12. Segmented/notched XP bar (RPG-style ticks) instead of a smooth fill.
13. Attribute gains animate (+1 FOCUS floats over the stat bar) when linked actions complete.
